Why This Segment

Investment Case

Structural Demand Drivers

Supply Scarcity in Prime Locations
Land constraints in Jardins, Pinheiros and Itaim Bibi create natural barriers to new supply. Completed assets in these sub-markets are virtually irreplaceable.
UHNW Domestic Capital Formation
Wealth concentration in São Paulo's financial and industrial elite drives sustained demand for R$15,000–25,000/m² assets independent of macro cycles.
Foreign Buyer Entry Opportunity
BRL depreciation vs. USD creates a favorable entry condition for international capital into São Paulo's most liquid premium sub-markets.
Record Supply Absorption
139,700 units launched in São Paulo in 2025 — an all-time record — were substantially absorbed, confirming deep structural demand across all price tiers.

Why Invest

Investment Thesis

01
Prime Location Premium
All three projects sit in SP's most expensive residential sub-markets (Jardins, Higienópolis, Pinheiros) where price comps range R$16,000–R$19,500/m².
02
Innovative Land-Swap Structure
FII Permuta exchanges land equity for fund units, giving investors direct GDV upside exposure while the corporate guarantee provides meaningful downside protection.
03
Guaranteed Minimum Return
Vitacon Participações S.A. provides a corporate MRG guarantee. If the fund underperforms at maturity, the developer covers the shortfall — a rare structural protection.
04

Monte Bravo has structured R$2.9bi across 35 operations. Deep relationships with developers, administrators and qualified investors ensure execution certainty.
05
Regulated CVM 160 Framework
Public offering under CVM Resolution 160, with Daycoval as independent administrator — institutional-grade governance with full qualified investor protections.
06
Compelling Risk / Return
